#### 1. **PROJECT.md** - The "What & Why"
**Purpose**: Single source of truth about what you're building

**Should contain**:
```markdown
# Finflow - Personal Wealth Hub

## What It Is
A premium personal wealth tracking platform that aggregates all your financial assets and liabilities into a single, beautiful dashboard:
- **Banks & Savings**: Manual or API-based bank tracking.
- **Investment Portfolios**: Stocks (Zerodha, IBKR, mStock) and Crypto (Binance, LUNO).
- **Insurance**: Tracking policy cash values and maturity dates.
- **Physical Assets**: Property (REITs, residential), Vehicles, and Gold.
- **Debts**: Loans, mortgages, and credit card utilization.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Real-time conversion across MYR, INR, USD, and SGD.

## Target Users
1. **Primary**: Personal investors tracking global portfolios (Malaysian/Indian context).
2. **Secondary**: Family members (shared net worth views with privacy controls).
3. **Tertiary**: Friends and community (broker plugin marketplace).

## Core Features
- **Portfolio Aggregation**: See everything in one place via read-only API connectors.
- **Multi-Currency Engine**: Real-time FX with smart formatting (₹1.2L / $1.2M).
- **Premium Design System**: State-of-the-art "Glassmorphism" UI better than Kubera or Wealthica.
- **Custom Plugin System**: A no-code engine allowing users to add any niche broker or bank API.

## Success Criteria
- **MVP**: Complete manual tracking + major broker read-only sync + basic dashboard.
- **Phase 2**: Family sharing and advanced debt payoff calculators.
- **Phase 3**: Community plugin marketplace live.

> [!NOTE]
> FinFlow is a **read-only** analytics dashboard. For active trading, users connect specialized third-party execution engines (like the ARUN Trading Bot).
```
